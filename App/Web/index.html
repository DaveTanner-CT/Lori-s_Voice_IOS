Help & Info

Talking: Tap any tile to say it out loud. A tile with a small number badge opens more choices inside it. Swipe left/right — or use the ‹ › arrows — for more pages. Tap the dark top bar to repeat the last phrase.
Typing: Tap ⌨️ Type at the top to open the keyboard, type a message, and tap 🔊 Speak.
Editing the board: Tap the 🔓 lock and enter the edit PIN. In edit mode you can add a tile (the dashed ＋ Add tile), edit a tile (the ✏️ on each one), drag tiles to reorder, add sub-tiles inside a tile, and delete tiles. Tap the lock again to leave edit mode. The communication board itself never requires a PIN and remains immediately available whenever the app opens.
Settings (⚙️, in edit mode): Change the board name, the greeting name, the color theme, tiles per page, the voice and speaking speed, and back up or restore the board.
Natural voice on iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → download an “Enhanced” voice, then choose it in ⚙️ → Voice.
Backup / copy to another device: ⚙️ → “Save board to a file”, then “Load board from a file” on the other device.

Data & Privacy

Where your changes are saved. Everything you set up — your tiles and words, the board name, the greeting, colors, voice, speaking speed, PIN, and notes — is saved locally on this device. It is not uploaded to a Lori's Voice server.
Nothing is sent automatically. There is no account and no sign-in. Lori's Voice does not upload your board, your words, or anything you type to a Lori's Voice server. There is no tracking and no advertising. A backup is shared only when you choose a destination for it.
Speaking. When you tap a tile or use the keyboard, the app uses your device's own built-in voice to say the words out loud. What you tap or type is not saved after it is spoken.
Keep a backup. Because everything lives on this one device, it is a good idea to keep a copy. In Settings, tap “Save board to a file.” If the device is reset, its local site/app data is cleared, or the installed app is removed, the saved board can be lost — a backup lets you restore it, or copy it to another device with “Load board from a file.”
Each device is separate. Every device keeps its own copy. To use the same board on another device, save it to a file here and load that file there.
