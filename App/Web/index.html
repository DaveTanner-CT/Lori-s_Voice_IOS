Help & Info

Quick help

Talk: Tap a tile once to speak. A numbered tile opens more choices. Tap the dark phrase bar to repeat the last phrase.
Type: Tap ⌨️ Type, enter a message, then tap 🔊 Speak.
More pages: Swipe left/right or use the ‹ › arrows.

Caregiver / editor help

Edit the board: Tap the 🔓 lock and enter the edit PIN. You can add, edit, reorder, or delete tiles and add sub-tiles. Tap the lock again to leave edit mode.
Settings: In edit mode, tap ⚙️ to change the board name, greeting, greeting icon, color theme, tiles per page, tile icon size, voice, speaking speed, and backup/restore options.
Natural voice: On iPad, go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices, download an Enhanced voice, then select it in Lori's Voice Settings.
Important: The communication board itself never requires a PIN and is immediately available whenever the app opens.

Data & Privacy

Where your changes are saved. Your board, tiles, words, photos, greeting, colors, voice, speaking speed, PIN, and device notes are stored in Lori's Voice's private local storage on this device. They are not uploaded to a Lori's Voice server.
No account, tracking, or advertising. Lori's Voice does not require an account or sign-in and does not send your board or typed messages to a Lori's Voice server. The app does not include advertising or analytics tracking.
Speaking stays on the device. Lori's Voice uses the device's built-in speech system. A message typed in Type to Talk is used for speaking and is not added to the communication board unless you separately create a tile for it.
Backups are user-controlled. A backup is created only when you choose “Save board to a file.” You decide where that file is saved or shared. Restoring a backup happens only when you choose “Load board from a file.”
Keep a backup. If the app is deleted, the device is erased, or local app data is lost, the board may be lost too. A saved backup lets you restore the board or copy it to another device.
Each device is separate. Lori's Voice does not automatically sync boards between devices.
