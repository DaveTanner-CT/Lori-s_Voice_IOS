# Lori's Voice Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 22, 2026**

Lori's Voice is a communication-board application developed by Scripting for Schools.

Lori's Voice does not require an account or sign-in. The application does not send a user's communication board, tile content, typed phrases, selected photos, PIN, or personal settings to Scripting for Schools or to a Lori's Voice server.

Board information and settings are stored locally on the user's device. Users may choose to create a backup file and save or share that file using Apple's standard iOS sharing tools. If a user chooses to share a backup, the destination selected by the user is responsible for handling that file.

Lori's Voice uses Apple's on-device speech services to speak words and phrases selected by the user. Lori's Voice does not maintain its own copy of those spoken phrases on a remote server.

Lori's Voice does not contain advertising and does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Users may optionally choose photos from their device to display on communication tiles. Those images are stored as part of the local communication board and are not uploaded by Lori's Voice to Scripting for Schools.
